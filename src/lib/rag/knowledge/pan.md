# PAN verification

The Income Tax e-filing portal lets a PAN holder authenticate their own PAN (know-your-PAN style flows). Entities such as financial institutions and government agencies use authorized channels — including empanelled agencies for bulk PAN verification under agreements — to verify name and status against PAN records.

## Fourth character semantics

The fourth character encodes holder class: P individual, C company, H HUF, F firm/LLP, A association of persons, T trust, B body of individuals, L local authority, J artificial juridical person, G government.

## Implication for procurement checks

A structurally valid PAN demonstrates correct transcription only. Name-and-status confirmation requires either the holder-assisted portal flow or an authorized verification service.

# Local content and Make in India orders

The Public Procurement (Preference to Make in India) Order 2017 requires bidders claiming preference to declare local content percentage and provide supporting certifications. GeM bids specify minimum local content thresholds and whether Class-I or Class-II local supplier status applies.

## Verification approach

Cross-check the local content declaration percentage against tender threshold, confirm margin-of-purchase conditions, and require item-wise local content breakup when values exceed thresholds specified in the order.

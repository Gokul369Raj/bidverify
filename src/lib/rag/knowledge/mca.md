# MCA master data service

View Company Master Data on MCA's portal exposes CIN-linked company name, registration date, category, class, authorized capital and registered address without login. Certified copies of incorporation documents require paid requests.

## Usage in bid verification

Match the CIN on the Certificate of Incorporation to master data: name spelling, status (Active/Strike-off), and incorporation date. Divergence between trading names and registered names is common and must be resolved via the registered name first.

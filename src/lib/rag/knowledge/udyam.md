# Udyam certificate and its dynamic QR code

Udyam registration certificates carry a dynamic QR code from which the enterprise's portal page and details can be accessed for verification. The certificate shows the Udyam Registration Number in the format UDYAM-AA-NN-NNNNNNN, enterprise type (Micro/Small/Medium), and official address.

## Verification procedure without API access

Scan or open the QR link to reach the Udyam portal verification page, confirm enterprise name, type and validity against the uploaded certificate. Manual verification through the portal's "Verify Udyam" facility uses the Udyam number.

## Enterprise type limits

Investment and turnover ceilings distinguish Micro, Small and Medium categories; certificates do not expire but enterprise classification may change upon re-registration of updated returns.
